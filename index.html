You are in orbit. Everything here — you, the enemy, every bolt you fire — falls around the black hole under real gravity. Nothing flies straight for long.

Burn prograde (toward the ⊕ marker) to raise the far side of your orbit. Burn retrograde to drop toward the hole. Lower orbits move faster.

The hole is cover. Nothing gets through it — not your bolts, not theirs, not a missile lock. Use it to break line of sight, or swing around it to catch a straggler on the far side. Stray too far out and a gravity wall hauls you back in.

Salvage & upgrades: destroyed ships leave wrecks in orbit for 2 minutes. Fly within 75 m to salvage their scrap. Between waves you get 2 minutes — rendezvous with Kepler Station (green marker). It holds a high, slow orbit. Get within 300 m while moving under 40 m/s relative to it and you dock automatically, or press B and the docking assist will fly the rendezvous for you. Spare missiles double as salvage drones: lock a wreck with E and fire, and the missile scoops up every wreck it flies through. Spend scrap on upgrades — but you can still be shot while docked.

The cyan line is your predicted path. If it turns red and ends at the hole, burn prograde now.

Missiles: press E with a hostile near your crosshair and hold it there until the lock completes, then press Q. They burn for 4 seconds, then fall ballistic and self-destruct — fire inside 1000 m.

Kills made while you are within 750 m of the singularity score DANGER CLOSE ×2. Chain kills to build your multiplier.
