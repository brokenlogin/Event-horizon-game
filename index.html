You are in orbit. Everything here — you, the enemy, every bolt you fire — falls around the black hole under real gravity. Nothing flies straight for long.

Burn prograde (toward the ⊕ marker) to raise the far side of your orbit. Burn retrograde to drop toward the hole. Lower orbits move faster.

The hole is cover. Nothing gets through it — not your bolts, not theirs, not a missile lock. Use it to break line of sight, or swing around it to catch a straggler on the far side. Stray too far out and a gravity wall hauls you back in.

Salvage & upgrades: destroyed ships leave wrecks in orbit for 2 minutes. Fly within 75 m to salvage their scrap. Between waves you get 2 minutes — rendezvous with Kepler Station (green marker). It holds a high, slow orbit. Get within 300 m while moving under 40 m/s relative to it and you dock automatically, or press B and the docking assist will fly the rendezvous for you. Spare missiles double as salvage drones: lock a wreck with E and fire, and the missile scoops up every wreck it flies through. Spend scrap on upgrades, Nanite Repair included (it slowly repairs your hull in flight), but you can still be shot while docked.

Multiplayer: up to 4 pilots. Pick a callsign, then choose LAN (same network: host a room or join with its code) or REMOTE (any networks: the host runs host-remote from the game folder and shares the code it prints). The host starts the flight. Waves grow with the crew (+100% hostiles and +60% upgrade prices per extra pilot), enemies hunt whoever is closest, and scrap and upgrades are shared. Shot down? A wingmate must dock and spend 100 scrap to revive you. If everyone is down, the run ends. The station has a docking clamp for each of you.

Difficulty: Easy — 30% fewer enemies, who deal 35% less damage, and you carry 3 missiles. Normal — standard waves, 2 missiles. Hard — 35% more enemies, who deal 30% more damage, and you carry just 1 missile. In multiplayer the host picks it in the waiting room.

The cyan line is your predicted path. If it turns red and ends at the hole, burn prograde now.

Missiles: press E with a hostile near your crosshair and hold it there until the lock completes, then press Q. They burn for 4 seconds, then fall ballistic and self-destruct. Lock range is 1000 m, plus 200 m per Missile Boosters level (doubled against the Dreadnought).

Dreadnought: every fifth wave an enemy fortress takes up a low orbit (about 1350 m) with a lighter escort. It has a lot of armour, six turrets that each cover one side of the hull, and a launcher in its prow that sends out seeker drones. Knock out the turrets to blunt it. Press B to have the autopilot park you 600 m behind it on the same orbit. Missiles lock onto it from 2000 m. Its hulk is worth 150 scrap.

MK2 ship: once Missile Racks and Hull Plating are both maxed, the station offers the MK2 refit for 300 scrap: +50% engine power, a new look, and you keep every upgrade. After that the upgrade list becomes the MK2 tech tree: 52 nodes across Weapons, Missiles, Defense and Engineering. It includes higher tiers of your upgrades and new abilities like the Railgun and Afterburner. Click a node to see it, then BUY.

New contacts: after the first boss, new ships join the waves. Lancers (wave 6) charge a beam: when the green aim line flashes, thrust across it to dodge. Sowers (wave 8) lay proximity mines along their orbit, which are safe to shoot from range. Aegis ships (wave 11) shield everything near them, so destroy them first.

Kills made while you are within 750 m of the singularity score DANGER CLOSE ×2. Chain kills to build your multiplier.
